# Data notes and disclaimer

## About this dashboard

This dashboard has been prepared using data collected from Australian Government
agencies and ministers subject to the *Freedom of Information Act 1982* (FOI Act).
Australian Government agencies and ministers are required to provide, among other
things, information about:

- the number of Freedom of Information (FOI) requests made to them
- the number of decisions they made granting, partially granting or refusing
  access, and the number and outcome of applications for internal review
- the timeliness of the decisions they made in relation to FOI requests made to
  them
- the number and outcome of requests to them to amend personal records
- charges they collected.

Supporting data are available for download at data.gov.au.

Individual agencies and ministers have custodianship and control over the data
entered by them into the FOI agency database and reported to the Office of the
Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) and are responsible for the
reliability and quality of the data contained in this dashboard. Requests to
access case management data in this dashboard should be directed to the agency
for which data is sought. Information on how to make such a request should be
available on the agency's "right to information" website page. The OAIC does not
hold agency case management data.

## Disclaimer on the use of agency FOI data

The OAIC is committed to open and transparent access to data about agencies' FOI
data. The OAIC makes every attempt to ensure the accuracy, currency and
reliability of data contained in this dashboard. The data used in this report has
been produced and processed from data provided by each agency and is considered
to be reliable, accurate and complete at the point of quarterly extraction. On
occasion, data is recorded or re-categorised on activities undertaken for prior
reporting periods after the conclusion of those reporting periods, which may
result in changes to previously reported historical data. The figures may also
differ from those in the OAIC's Annual Report. The OAIC, its employees or agents
do not accept responsibility for the use any person makes of the data it provides
on this section of its website.

The OAIC does not accept any responsibility or liability for material contained
on third party sites that reference the information on this website or for the
use any person makes of such third-party information. The OAIC does not monitor
these websites and makes no representations in relation to the quality or
accuracy of the information on third-party websites.

## Notes on the data

Agencies who have changed names, but have materially maintained the same
responsibilities, are represented under their most recent name, including for
periods where their former names applied. Agencies that have been created because
of Machinery of Government changes that combine responsibilities of several
superseded agencies or where responsibilities are materially different to the
superseded agencies, are represented as new entities.

Data is presented on a quarterly and financial year basis. For example (Q1)
represents the July–September financial year quarter, and a year represents the
July–June financial year period.

References to "personal information" may include personal information relating to
the applicant or personal information of another person.
